Reading your notes over and over is the least effective way to study. We've compiled the most scientifically proven methods to help you move info from short-term to long-term memory.

Core Techniques

01

Active Recall

Testing your brain by forcing it to retrieve information. Don't look at the answer; pull it out of your head.

BEST FOR Flashcards, practice papers, and blurting.
02

Spaced Repetition

Reviewing information at increasing intervals (1 day, 1 week, 1 month) to fight the "Forgetting Curve."

BEST FOR Long-term memory retention for Science & History.
03

The Feynman Method

Explaining a complex concept in the simplest terms possible—as if you were teaching a 5-year-old.

BEST FOR Understanding difficult Maths or Physics theories.
04

Pomodoro Sprints

25 minutes of deep work, followed by a 5-minute break. This keeps your focus high and prevents burnout.

BEST FOR Managing long revision days and avoiding distraction.
