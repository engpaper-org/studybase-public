Exam Technique Hub

Master the Command Words.

Don't lose marks by misinterpreting the question. Learn exactly what exam boards like AQA and Edexcel expect from you.

📝

Describe

Recall facts, events, or processes. You don't need to give reasons why.

Example: "Describe the structure of an atom."
🔍

Explain

Give reasons why something happens. Use words like "because" or "therefore".

Example: "Explain why sodium is reactive."
⚖️

Evaluate

Look at both sides (pros/cons) and give a justified conclusion.

Example: "Evaluate the use of wind power."
🔄

Compare

Identify similarities and differences between two or more things.

Example: "Compare eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells."
🔢

Calculate

Work out a numerical answer. Always show your working stages.

Example: "Calculate the mean mass of the samples."
💡

Suggest

Apply your knowledge to a new situation. There may be more than one right answer.

Example: "Suggest why the experiment was repeated."
💬

Discuss

Explore a topic by looking at different viewpoints or factors.

Example: "Discuss the impact of climate change on farmers."
🎯

Identify

Briefly name or select a piece of information from a source or text.

Example: "Identify one hazard shown in the diagram."
🛡️

Justify

Give evidence or reasons to support an answer or a specific point of view.

Example: "Justify your choice of sampling method."
