Personal Statement Anatomy

The ABC Rule
draft_v1.docx
1. The Hook (20%)

"Why this subject? Don't use quotes like 'Since I was young...'. Instead, discuss a specific problem, book, or moment that sparked genuine academic curiosity."

2. Super-Curriculars (60%) - The Meat

"This is where you prove you can study at a high level. Use the ABC rule:"

  • A (Activity): "I read 'Thinking Fast and Slow'..."
  • B (Benefit): "...which introduced me to the concept of cognitive biases..."
  • C (Course): "...leading me to explore how this applies to economic decision making in my A-Level studies."
3. Extra-Curriculars (20%)

"Keep this brief. Focus on transferable skills. How does being Cricket Captain make you a better History student? (Time management, leadership)."
