Device Compatibility Notice

Understanding your
Device Restriction

To maintain a secure and reliable experience for all users, StudyBase only supports a specific range of devices and system environments. If your current device falls outside those supported parameters, certain parts of the platform may not function as expected or access may be restricted.

Why might my device be restricted?

StudyBase is designed around a controlled set of device characteristics and operating environments that allow us to maintain consistent functionality and security across the platform. Some devices, browsers, or operating systems may not provide the capabilities required for the application to run safely or reliably.

In these cases, the system may prevent access to protect both the user and the underlying infrastructure. Unsupported environments can sometimes introduce instability, compatibility issues, or security concerns that could negatively affect the experience of other users on the platform.

What can I do?

1

Try another supported device

If possible, access StudyBase using a different device or system environment that meets the platform requirements.

2

Ensure your system is up to date

Outdated operating systems or browsers may sometimes trigger compatibility restrictions. Updating your device may resolve the issue.

3

Contact support if needed

If you believe your device should be supported but access is still restricted, our support team can review the situation and provide further guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are some devices unsupported?

Some devices may not provide the required security features or system capabilities that StudyBase relies on. Restricting unsupported environments helps protect both user data and the stability of the platform.

Can this restriction be bypassed?

Device restrictions are applied automatically by the system and cannot usually be overridden. They exist to maintain security, reliability, and performance across the platform.
