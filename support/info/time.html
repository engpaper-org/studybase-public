Scheduled overnight shutdowns have been removed. StudyBase services should stay available unless an administrator manually enables a temporary outage for urgent maintenance.

Why am I seeing this page?

RevisionBase performs Service Availability overnight to keep the platform stable, fast, and reliable during the busiest parts of the day. During this time, our systems may process updates, refresh internal services, carry out health checks, and roll out fixes or improvements.

If access is blocked, it should be because of an account, device, or administrator-controlled service state rather than the time of day.

What can still affect access?

1

Administrator service controls

The site team can still temporarily disable services manually if urgent maintenance is required.

2

Account or device restrictions

Some account and device checks may still restrict access where required by the current security rules.

3

Refresh and retry

If you saw an old overnight message, refresh the page so your browser loads the latest scripts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there still an overnight shutdown?

Access normally returns automatically from the service is restored. In rare cases, maintenance may take slightly longer if we are deploying important fixes.

Why not leave the site online overnight?

Taking the platform offline overnight gives our infrastructure time to recover, process updates, and reduce background stress on the system.

Do I need to do anything?

No. Once the maintenance period ends, the site will become available again as normal.
