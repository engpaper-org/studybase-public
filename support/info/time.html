Scheduled Maintenance

StudyBase is currently
in Maintenance Mode

To help keep our site and infrastructure up and running smoothly, StudyBase temporarily shuts down each night between 11:00pm and 4:00am. This helps reduce stress on the system and gives us time to deploy updates, improvements, and bug fixes ready for the next day. We are sorry for any inconvenience this may cause.

Why is the site unavailable?

StudyBase performs scheduled maintenance overnight to keep the platform stable, fast, and reliable during the busiest parts of the day. During this time, our systems may process updates, refresh internal services, carry out health checks, and roll out fixes or improvements.

This maintenance window helps protect the platform from unnecessary strain and ensures everything is ready for students the following morning. Full access will automatically return once the maintenance period ends at 4:00am.

What happens during maintenance?

1

System updates are applied

Important improvements, fixes, and optimisations are pushed so the platform is ready for the next day.

2

Infrastructure load is reduced

Temporarily shutting down overnight helps reduce strain on our systems and improves long-term reliability.

3

Services are prepared for the morning

Caches, data, and key systems are refreshed so everything is running properly when access reopens.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will access return?

Access normally returns automatically from 4:00am. In rare cases, maintenance may take slightly longer if we are deploying important fixes.

Why not leave the site online overnight?

Taking the platform offline overnight gives our infrastructure time to recover, process updates, and reduce background stress on the system.

Do I need to do anything?

No. Once the maintenance period ends, the site will become available again as normal.

Thank you for your patience

We appreciate your understanding while we keep StudyBase secure, stable, and ready for the next day.
