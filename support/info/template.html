Why is my account restricted?

Account restrictions occur automatically when our system flags behavior that violates our community guidelines or terms of service. This is usually done to protect the integrity of the open-source materials and ensure a fair environment for all students.

Common reasons include automated scraping of predicted papers, sharing premium exemplar essays on third-party sites, or abusive behavior in the global study hub.

Steps to Restore Access

1

Review the Guidelines

Ensure you are familiar with our fair-use policy regarding A* exemplars and exam materials.

2

Submit an Appeal

Contact our moderation team using the form below with a brief explanation of the activity in question.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an appeal take?

Our team typically reviews appeals within 48 hours. During peak exam seasons (April-May), this may take up to 72 hours.

Can I still use the Focus Room?

Yes. Most restrictions only apply to downloading premium resources or commenting. The Focus Room and Pomodoro timers remain active.
