If you believe your account or device was banned in error, you can submit an appeal below. Please keep your explanation clear, honest, and specific.

Before you appeal

Appeals are reviewed manually. Sending a short or vague message may delay the process, so it helps to explain exactly what happened and why you believe the ban should be reviewed.

1

Explain what happened

Describe the activity around the time of the ban in as much useful detail as you can.

2

Be honest and specific

If you made a mistake, say so clearly. Honest appeals are easier to review fairly.

3

Avoid repeat submissions

Submitting multiple appeals for the same case may slow things down rather than speed them up.

What to include

The email or account you were using

Whether the device is shared with anyone else

Anything unusual that may have triggered the system

Why you believe the ban should be lifted or reviewed

Appeal timing

Review times may vary

Appeals are reviewed as quickly as possible, but response times may be longer during busy periods or where additional checks are needed.

Frequently asked questions

What happens after I submit an appeal?

Your submission is sent for review. A moderator or admin may assess the details you provided alongside security records and account activity.

Can I appeal more than once?

You should normally wait for the result of your first appeal before sending another one. Multiple repeated submissions may slow the process.

What if the form is unavailable?

If the form is temporarily unavailable, please try again later. This page will show a clear message when the appeal system is offline.
