Account enforcement

This account is banned

A ban blocks authenticated StudyBase services and remains in place until an administrator removes it.

There is no user appeal process. Repeated login attempts, replacement accounts, or attempts to bypass the restriction may be recorded as further abuse.

What is affected

  • Weekly sessions and protected account APIs.
  • Protected resource access and account-linked applications.
  • New sessions for the banned username.

Administrative removal

Only an authorised StudyBase administrator using the restricted dashboard can remove a ban. Removal is discretionary and is not available through a public form or endpoint.
