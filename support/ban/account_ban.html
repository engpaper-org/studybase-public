A ban blocks authenticated StudyBase services and remains in place until an administrator removes it.

You may request a human review. Use the support contact page and include your username, the approximate time access stopped, and any relevant context. Do not include your password or session token. Repeated bypass attempts may be recorded as further abuse.

What is affected

  • Weekly sessions and protected account APIs.
  • Protected resource access and account-linked applications.
  • New sessions for the banned username.

Administrative review

Only an authorised StudyBase administrator can remove a ban. A review considers the recorded signal and any context you provide.
