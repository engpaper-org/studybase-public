How to survive

The game starts slowly for the first few seconds so you can see what is happening.

1

Click the glowing orbs on the focus-break screen to score points.

2

Watch the teacher meter at the top. When it gets high, danger is close.

3

Switch to the study tab before the meter fills. You get a save bonus if you time it well.

4

Press Space to quickly swap tabs, or use the two buttons at the bottom.
