The A-Level paper loop.

The simplest revision loop is still the most useful: choose a paper, attempt it honestly, mark it carefully, then repair one weakness before the next attempt.

1. Choose

Pick a paper or section that matches your current course and exam board.

2. Attempt

Use real timing when possible. Untimed work is useful only when you know why you are doing it.

3. Repair

Log one weak topic and turn it into recall, explanation or a short drill.
