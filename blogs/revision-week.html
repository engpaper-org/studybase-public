Build a revision week that actually changes your next mark.

A useful week includes paper attempts, marking time and targeted repair blocks. It should not be a list of subjects with no output.

Anchor with papers

Schedule the paper or timed section first, then add the repair around it.

Protect marking time

Marking is where the evidence appears. Do not squeeze it into the final five minutes.

Repeat weak topics

Bring the same weak topic back later in the week so it does not vanish.
