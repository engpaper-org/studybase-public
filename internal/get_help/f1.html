About this check-in

Why am I seeing this?

StudyBase uses the gender saved on this device to choose this check-in version. Message views, support-link opens, explanation opens and time before continuing help your browser decide which messages may be useful and how often to show them.

This interaction history stays in this browser under get_help_data. It is not sent to StudyBase or stored on StudyBase servers, and it is not used to diagnose a mental-health condition. StudyBase does not receive or read anything you say to an external support provider.
