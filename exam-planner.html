Build a week that actually improves marks.

Use this route as a planning board: split your week into timed attempts, marking blocks, weak-topic repair and light recall.

Monday to Wednesday

Short topic repair, formula recall and one timed paper section.

Thursday to Friday

Full timed attempt, then mark with a real mark scheme and write down the costliest errors.

Weekend

Retry the weak topic, update your tracker and choose the next paper.
