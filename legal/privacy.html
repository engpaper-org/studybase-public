1. Scope

This Privacy Notice applies to StudyBase pages, public resources, account pages, protected-resource flows, revision tools, support/report routes, legal pages, embedded viewers, analytics scripts, safety systems, and connected infrastructure.

StudyBase is commonly used by students and is likely to be accessed by children. We therefore aim to provide high-privacy defaults, collect only data needed for requested features, and keep optional analytics off unless the user actively enables it.

1.1 Data controller

For the purposes of UK data protection law, the controller is StudyBase, the UK-based operator of this service. Contact the controller through the Support Centre and select Privacy / data request. That route can be used without enabling analytics. We may ask for proportionate information to verify an account-related request.

2. Browser storage

StudyBase uses cookies, local storage and session storage so pages can remember privacy choices, account state, session expiry, device codes, display names, server-synchronised settings, safety toggles, active time, break reminders, resource state, and similar site behaviour.

  • Privacy-choice records include studybase_consent_choice and compatibility keys. They record the notice version, necessary-storage status, optional analytics choice, decision time and expiry. They do not contain a generated tracking identifier.
  • Account storage contains a non-secret session marker, session expiry, and a cached copy of the signed-in user's account profile, including username, first name, date of birth, gender, status, and settings. Passwords and eligibility email addresses are not stored in browser storage.
  • Usage records may include local recent items, search preferences, tool choices, and the get_help_data record used to avoid repeating wellbeing messages and to adjust their local frequency. That record may include whether a message was marked helpful or not useful, topics hidden by the user, support-link opens, explanation opens, and time before continuing. Wellbeing interaction records remain in the browser, are not uploaded to StudyBase, and are cleared when the account is signed out.

Clearing browser storage may remove local preferences and may sign you out, but it does not automatically delete server-side account records, hashed eligibility records, access logs, or support records. Use the account deletion control for server-side deletion.

2.1 Cookies and similar technologies

Browser storage is governed by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), as amended. Necessary storage is used without consent only where it is essential to provide a feature you request, transmit a communication, remember this privacy choice, or apply proportionate security. Optional analytics is disabled until you actively allow it.

  • StudyBase privacy choice (local storage; necessary; up to 6 months): records whether analytics is allowed so we can respect your choice.
  • Account session and security (first-party cookies and local/session storage; necessary; until the next Monday at 00:01 Europe/London): authenticates a requested account session, prevents misuse, and remembers account state.
  • Requested tools and preferences (local/session storage; necessary or preference functionality; until cleared or no longer needed): saves work and settings on the device when you use those features.
  • Google Analytics (_ga and _ga_*; optional; normally up to 2 years): Google Ireland Limited helps measure visits, pages and aggregate usage. Advertising storage, Google Signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Google may process data outside the UK under its applicable transfer safeguards.

You can accept, reject or customise optional analytics from the first-layer banner. No optional category is pre-selected. Reopen Cookie settings at any time. Withdrawing consent deletes accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded analytics library. It does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.

3. Account, login, access, and report data

If you create an account, StudyBase may process your name, date of birth, gender selection, username, password, session state, login timestamps, IP address, user-agent information, and resource access state. A readable eligibility email is used transiently for the check but is not retained: the Worker stores a keyed cryptographic hash so it can detect previous use. Passwords are salted and hashed on the server; readable passwords are not stored.

New account signup currently accepts a male or female gender selection. StudyBase keeps that selection private from public profiles and uses the cached value on the device to choose the matching local wellbeing check-in experience. Interaction with those check-ins is processed in browser storage and is not sent to StudyBase servers.

If you submit support forms, reports, contact messages, APG requests, or similar inputs, StudyBase may process the information you choose to send, plus page context and technical metadata needed to review or operate the request.

New sessions are bound to a keyed browser fingerprint. Requests made with an account cookie from an unapproved origin are refused and a minimal security event may be retained for up to two days. Presenting a valid bearer session token from an unapproved origin triggers an automated account ban and session revocation because it indicates that the secret token has been extracted from its protected cookie context. A user may ask for human review through the published support contact route.

If you use Friends & Social, another signed-in user who enters your exact username may see your first name, username, and profile picture before sending a request. Pending requests and accepted friendships are stored against account UUIDs, rather than mutable usernames, together with the time a request was created. Friends do not receive your date of birth, gender, eligibility-address hash, email address, password information, login history, or IP information through the friends API. You can disable new incoming friend requests from the Friends privacy control; existing friends and requests remain available for you to manage until you remove, accept, decline, or cancel them.

StudyBase also records a short-lived daily friend-request count to enforce the limit of 10 newly sent requests per day. The browser keeps a matching local daily count to provide immediate feedback, but the server-side limit remains authoritative.

StudyShop stores each account's StudyBucks balance, daily and weekly quest progress, claimed rewards, milestone claims, and lifetime economy statistics. Quest progress may include visit dates, up to ten daily and fifty weekly resource identifiers, daily-quest claims, friend requests, and newly accepted friendships. It does not store protected resource URLs in quest records. Quest progress and rewards are checked by the API rather than relying only on browser data.

A user may send StudyBucks to an accepted friend. The service records the amount, direction, time, a transfer reference, daily transfer usage, and updated balances. Counterparty profile details are not included in the self-service privacy export. Transfers require a five-second confirmation warning and are irreversible after confirmation.

When a user opens a StudyShop card pack, the API checks the account balance, selects a card using the published rarity weighting, deducts the pack price, and stores the card identifier and number of copies against the account. The service also keeps aggregate counts of packs and cards opened. A card count is stored only up to 100 copies and is displayed as 99+ at that limit. Unowned card artwork is not requested by the Locker collection view.

4. Worker and API processing

StudyBase pages may send requests to API workers and service endpoints configured in siteConfig.js. These may include account state, login state, protected-resource access, resource lists, top resource cards, eligibility checks, APG alert data, report routing, and update checks.

Requests to those services may include credentials, transient eligibility details, resource identifiers, page context, browser metadata, verification responses where configured, and other operational values required to run the requested feature.

5. Analytics, safety, and monitoring

Cookie consent is separate from acceptance of the Terms and from the lawful bases used to provide account features. Rejecting optional analytics does not sign you out, block core pages, or prevent requested account and security services. We store the preference on this device and do not send a consent log containing the page URL or user agent to a third-party logging endpoint.

Full service features may use Google Apps Script, Cloudflare Turnstile, local safety filters, time checks, error checks, update checks, endpoint status notices, and abuse-prevention logic where required to provide or protect the requested feature.

Study tools (timer, planner, checklists, flashcards) store all data locally in your browser using localStorage and sessionStorage. These tools do not send data to StudyBase servers unless you explicitly choose to link activity to a logged-in account.

Search-safety and text-filtering systems may normalise input, compare it with blocked references, store local violation counts, show warnings, or temporarily disable inputs. Automated systems can produce false positives or false negatives.

Account login events are checked for irregular patterns, including unusually broad use across hashed IP addresses. A threshold may temporarily restrict access for security review. It does not by itself permanently ban an account. Permanent bans require an administrator. StudyBase does not provide an internal ban appeal form, but applicable statutory rights concerning personal data and significant automated decisions remain available. IP and user-agent values used by the account service are stored as keyed hashes rather than readable values.

6. Third-party services

StudyBase may load third-party fonts, scripts, CDNs, forms, embedded resources, Turnstile challenges, external websites, or linked educational materials. Those services may receive ordinary browser request data such as IP-related information, user agent, referrer, cookies, and usage events.

StudyBase does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or embedded destinations.

6.1 Sellbase checkout and purchase fulfilment

Optional StudyBase plans are not currently available to purchase. When purchasing is enabled, checkout and payment processing will be provided through Sellbase.gg. Information entered on Sellbase is processed under the privacy information and terms presented by Sellbase at checkout.

Payment details entered through Sellbase are not submitted to StudyBase pages, and StudyBase does not collect or store full payment-card details. To activate, support, and verify a purchase, StudyBase may receive or record the limited fulfilment information made available through the Sellbase integration, such as the purchased product, purchase status, access period, order or transaction reference, and the StudyBase account identifier needed to apply access. The exact fields will be described at the point purchasing becomes available.

Sellbase may independently process ordinary checkout and payment information, including information you submit directly and technical request information. Its retention, cookies, payment-provider relationships, and any international transfers are governed by the notices shown through its service. Users should review those notices before purchasing.

StudyBase processes purchase and fulfilment records where necessary to perform the purchase contract, provide support, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain accounting records, and comply with legal obligations. Records are retained only for as long as required for access fulfilment, disputes, security, accounting, tax, and other applicable legal requirements.

If an AI Credit Pack is purchased, StudyBase may store the account's credit allocation, remaining balance, purchase reference, credit adjustments, and usage deductions associated with sending AI messages. AI message content and related technical metadata may be processed to provide the requested AI feature, apply safety controls, investigate misuse, and calculate credit use. Users should not include sensitive personal information in AI messages. Any additional AI provider or retention details will be shown before the feature becomes available.

7. Lawful bases for processing

Under UK GDPR we rely on the following lawful bases for the processing activities described:

  • Contract — account creation, login, eligibility checks, protected resource access, optional-plan fulfilment, and core service delivery.
  • Consent — optional analytics and other non-essential cookies or similar technologies. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — proportionate abuse prevention, security monitoring, error logging, and maintaining the safety of the community (e.g. time tracking and banned-word filters).
  • Legal obligation — responding to valid legal requests or complying with applicable UK laws.

Where we process special category data (if ever), we will only do so with explicit consent or another condition under Article 9 UK GDPR.

8. Data retention

  • Local browser data: until you clear it or the browser removes it.
  • Pending signup records: up to 48 hours, unless used sooner.
  • Sessions: until the next Monday at 00:01 Europe/London, unless revoked or the account is deleted sooner.
  • Account profile: while the account is active. Password-confirmed self-service deletion revokes sessions and removes the account record, username index, keyed eligibility record, related pending signup data, friendships, pending friend requests, and friend-request rate records.
  • Friendships and pending requests: until a user removes, declines, or cancels them, or until either related account is deleted. Daily request-limit records expire shortly after the relevant day.
  • StudyShop balances, card collection identifiers and copy counts, milestone claims, lifetime statistics, and recent transfer history: while the account remains active. Daily quest records expire within approximately three days, weekly records within approximately 15 days, and transfer-completion safeguards within approximately 30 days. Account deletion clears the balance, card collection, and associated StudyShop records.
  • Account security login events: up to 30 days.
  • Deduplicated off-site request security events: up to two days. An enforcement record linked to an account is retained while the restriction remains and as reasonably needed to review it.
  • Support and report records: retained as long as needed to resolve the matter and for a reasonable period thereafter for audit and safety reasons.
  • Purchase and fulfilment records: for the access period and as long as reasonably required for support, disputes, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, and other legal obligations.
  • AI credit balances and deduction records: while credits remain usable and for a reasonable period afterwards to resolve balance disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain required transaction records.
  • Privacy choices on this device: up to six months, then we ask again. Analytics cookies may last up to two years unless deleted or consent is withdrawn sooner.

We regularly review and delete or anonymise data that is no longer required.

9. Your UK GDPR rights

Subject to the conditions and exemptions in UK data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed — this policy and any specific notices at the point of collection.
  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete data completed.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to have your personal data deleted in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your data in certain cases.
  • Right to data portability — to receive certain data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to have it transmitted to another controller.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing (we do not do direct marketing).
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — to receive information about significant automated decisions and obtain human intervention where UK data-protection law requires it. StudyBase automation can impose only a temporary security review, not a permanent ban.

You can delete your account and revoke all sessions from account settings. For access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or review of a security restriction, submit a request via the Support Centre. We will respond within the time limits required by law (usually one month, subject to lawful extensions).

Privacy rights are not conditional on agreeing with this notice. You may use the service with optional analytics disabled.

10. UK privacy law

This Privacy Notice is intended to be interpreted under applicable UK privacy and data protection law, including UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and relevant amendments made by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

Where those laws give users rights that cannot be removed by this policy, those rights continue to apply. The UK Information Commissioner's Office is the supervisory authority for UK data protection matters.

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer or data subject.
