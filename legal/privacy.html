Privacy Policy

How StudyBase handles data.

This policy explains browser storage, account information, consent records, analytics, reports, protected-resource checks, worker API requests, and third-party services used by StudyBase.

Version PRIVACY-2026.05.27

Last updated: 27 May 2026

Scope Browser storage Account data Workers and APIs Analytics Third parties Choices UK law

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to StudyBase pages, public resources, account pages, protected-resource flows, revision tools, support/report routes, legal pages, embedded viewers, analytics scripts, safety systems, and connected infrastructure.

StudyBase is commonly used by students. Users under 18 should use the service with permission from a parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult where required.

2. Browser storage

StudyBase uses local storage and session storage so pages can remember consent, account state, session expiry, device codes, display names, settings, quick-login preferences, search preferences, recent history, favourites, safety toggles, active time, break reminders, resource state, and similar site behaviour.

  • Consent records may include site_consent_granted and consent_uid.
  • Account records may include username, password-like login values, email, device code, session expiry, and quick-login state.
  • Usage records may include local recent items, favourites, search preferences, wellbeing timing, and tool choices.

Clearing browser storage may remove local preferences and may sign you out, but it does not automatically delete server-side account records, approved-email records, access logs, or support records.

3. Account, login, access, and report data

If you use account features, StudyBase may process usernames, passwords or password-like values, device identifiers, account email, profile names, session state, approved-email status, login timestamps, and resource access state.

If you submit support forms, reports, contact messages, APG requests, or similar inputs, StudyBase may process the information you choose to send, plus page context and technical metadata needed to review or operate the request.

4. Worker and API processing

StudyBase pages may send requests to API workers and service endpoints configured in site.config.json. These may include account state, login state, protected-resource access, resource lists, top resource cards, approved-email checks, APG alert data, report routing, and update checks.

Requests to those services may include credentials, email/access details, resource identifiers, page context, browser metadata, verification responses where configured, and other operational values required to run the requested feature.

5. Consent, analytics, safety, and monitoring

StudyBase may use Google Analytics, Google Apps Script, Cloudflare Turnstile, local safety filters, time checks, error checks, update checks, endpoint status notices, and abuse-prevention logic.

Consent logging may send a generated consent identifier, consent action, page URL, and user-agent information to a configured logging endpoint. Analytics consent is controlled by the consent state stored in the browser.

Search-safety and text-filtering systems may normalise input, compare it with blocked references, store local violation counts, show warnings, or temporarily disable inputs. Automated systems can produce false positives or false negatives.

6. Third-party services

StudyBase may load third-party fonts, scripts, CDNs, analytics, forms, embedded resources, Turnstile challenges, external websites, or linked educational materials. Those services may receive ordinary browser request data such as IP-related information, user agent, referrer, cookies, and usage events.

StudyBase does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or embedded destinations.

7. Rights and choices

Where applicable under UK data protection law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and similar privacy rights in relation to personal data processed by or for StudyBase.

You can remove many local values by clearing browser storage. Account deletion, approved-email records, access records, and server-side records are handled by request to the site administrator where available and legally appropriate.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should stop using StudyBase.

8. UK privacy law

This Privacy Policy is intended to be interpreted under applicable UK privacy and data protection law, including UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and PECR where relevant.

Where those laws give users rights that cannot be removed by this policy, those rights continue to apply. The UK Information Commissioner's Office is the supervisory authority for UK data protection matters.
