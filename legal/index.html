Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to StudyBase as a whole, including its public pages, account areas, legal notices, support and reporting routes, resource database, embedded resource viewers, revision tools, analytics scripts, APIs, moderation systems, and connected infrastructure.

By visiting, loading, browsing, interacting with, or continuing to use StudyBase, and where shown by pressing Agree on a consent prompt or continuing through an account or protected-resource flow, you acknowledge the privacy and data-handling practices described here.

Definitions, scope and age guidance

In this policy, "StudyBase", "the service", "we", "us", and "our" mean the StudyBase platform and its operator. "You" and "user" mean any person accessing or using any part of the service. The service is primarily intended for students and educational users, including GCSE and A-Level users.

Users under 18 should use the service with permission from a parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult. Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal, confidential, medical, or financial information into any free-text field unless it is clearly requested for a specific purpose.

Privacy information summary

StudyBase may process limited personal data and device-related information for security, access control, abuse prevention, analytics, feature operation, and user convenience. Depending on how you use the service, this may include browser information, request metadata, page URLs, material identifiers, browser-storage values, report submissions, consent events, access state, and session or membership status.

What StudyBase stores on your device

StudyBase uses local storage and, where relevant, session storage to remember consent, appearance settings, account and device identifiers, session-expiry values, search and usage preferences, saved favourites, recent actions, wellbeing timing, paper or subject choices, and safety toggles.

  • Consent records may include `site_consent_granted` and a generated `consent_uid`.
  • Account-related storage may include username, password-like values, and device values used to request protected resources.
  • Preference storage may include dark mode, search-history saving, recent-history saving, quick-login, and content-warning settings.
  • Activity storage may include active time, break reminders, favourites, recent resource behaviour, and idle-unload state.

These values are primarily stored locally on your own device rather than in a single conventional central account database. They may remain until they expire, are overwritten, are removed by browser behaviour, or are manually cleared by you.

Technical data collected automatically

When you access StudyBase, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the service, its hosting or infrastructure providers, or integrated scripts. This can include IP-related request data, browser type, user agent, approximate device type, referring page, request timing, viewed page URL, and similar diagnostic or security-related information.

Data you submit directly

If StudyBase provides report forms, support inputs, account-related forms, display-name fields, search fields, override inputs, or similar user-controlled text areas, information you choose to type there may be processed for the purpose of operating that feature. This can include display names, report reasons, support messages, search terms, manually entered codes, and other content you voluntarily submit.

Login, account, webhook and Apps Script data flows

StudyBase may transmit login-related data through multiple connected systems in order to operate account access, validate sessions, record login history, support abuse review, and maintain service administration.

  • Discord webhooks may be used to transmit login-related data and associated context for operational handling, alerts, logging, or internal review.
  • Google Apps Script endpoints may be used to transmit login-related data, store login-history records, and store anonymous or pseudonymous resource-usage records.
  • Where Google Forms are used for contact, report, support, or other submission flows, submitted information may be forwarded into Google Apps Script processing or connected storage flows.

Depending on the feature, this information may include usernames, device identifiers, login timestamps, page context, browser or request metadata, submitted form content, resource identifiers, and other operational context required for service administration, support, integrity, or review.

What StudyBase sends to its own services

StudyBase pages call platform endpoints for configuration, account or session status, resource listings, protected resource access, top-card content, state checks, reports, updates, and alerts. Depending on the page and feature used, requests may include your account username, stored password-like value, device code, requested material identifier, Turnstile token, current page URL, search text, or similar information needed for that feature to work.

Consent, cookies and similar technologies

StudyBase uses browser storage technologies including local storage and similar browser-side access mechanisms. For transparency, these should be treated in a similar way to cookies where applicable. StudyBase may also use scripts, tags, and consent-controlled analytics settings to manage functionality, permissions, and service measurement.

Consent status and consent logging

StudyBase may store a consent state on your device, including a flag indicating that consent has been granted, together with a locally generated consent identifier for repeat recognition and compliance logging. When consent is accepted, StudyBase may also transmit limited consent-related information such as a pseudonymous consent identifier, the current page URL, and browser user-agent information to a third-party logging endpoint for audit or compliance purposes.

Analytics, consent mode and measurement

StudyBase uses Google Analytics with consent mode. Before consent, analytics-related storage is set to denied. After you press Agree, StudyBase may update Google consent settings, enable analytics storage, and send page and usage data through Google tags.

  • The consent log may send a generated user ID, a consent marker, the current page URL, and your browser user agent.
  • Material analytics may send a material identifier from the page query string to a Google Apps Script endpoint.
  • Google services operate under their own terms and privacy policies as well as this legal notice.

Third parties and external services used by StudyBase

StudyBase may rely on third-party providers for analytics, logging, CDN delivery, scripts, consent-related functionality, security checks, hosting, forms, fonts, serverless functions, and access infrastructure.

  • Google services may be used for analytics, consent logging, resource analytics, and embedded forms.
  • Google Apps Script may be used for login-related transmission, identifiable login-history storage, consent logging, and anonymous or pseudonymous resource-usage storage.
  • Google Forms may be used to collect user-submitted information before forwarding or connecting that information to Apps Script processing flows.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile may be used on protected flows to verify requests and reduce abuse.
  • Cloudflare and related infrastructure may route, secure, filter, cache, proxy, or otherwise process traffic between you and StudyBase services.
  • Discord webhooks may be used for internal operational transmission of login-related or abuse-review-related information.
  • CDN-hosted libraries, fonts, and external assets may receive standard browser request data when loaded.
  • Embedded or linked third-party resources remain outside StudyBase's direct control.

Embedded content, redirects and external destinations

Some StudyBase features open external pages in a new tab, redirect you to another domain, or embed a destination inside a viewer frame. When that happens, the destination site may process your IP address, browser metadata, referrer information, page activity, and anything you submit there. StudyBase does not control the privacy practices, cookies, account systems, moderation rules, or security posture of those external services.

Search safety, moderation and automated filtering

StudyBase may use automated search-safety and text-filtering systems. In the current implementation, search input may be normalised, hashed, compared against locally loaded blocked references, and actioned automatically where a match is detected. The service may store local violation counts, display warnings, disable search after repeated violations, and allow limited local override behaviour in some cases.

These systems are automated and may produce false positives or false negatives. They are provided on a best-efforts basis only.

Reports, moderation and anti-abuse processing

StudyBase may process report submissions, moderation-related information, timestamps, page context, session or device context, and technical request data for moderation, anti-spam, abuse prevention, integrity protection, and internal review. Users must not submit false reports, malicious payloads, exploit strings, or spam through these routes.

Children, school use and institution networks

StudyBase may be accessed on school, college, employer, library, home, or otherwise monitored networks. Those institutions or providers may separately monitor traffic, browsing, DNS requests, filtering events, or device activity. StudyBase is not responsible for third-party monitoring carried out by your institution, employer, parent, ISP, or device manager.

User rights and choices

Where applicable under UK data protection law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and similar privacy rights in relation to personal data processed by or for StudyBase. Because StudyBase uses substantial client-side storage, many values are directly viewable or removable by you in your own browser settings.

If you clear site storage, switch browser, use private browsing, reset the device, or block scripts, some features may stop working or may reset, including consent state, account state, recent history, personalisation, and access continuity. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not continue to use StudyBase.
