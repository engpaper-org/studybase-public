Token Policy

How StudyBase tokens work.

Tokens are an account-linked digital balance for selected StudyBase features. This policy explains top-ups, product codes, spending, failed requests, expiry, account records, and misuse.

Last updated: 20 May 2026

1. What tokens are

StudyBase tokens are a digital account balance used to access selected StudyBase tools, services, requests, resource features, premium features, or other token-supported functionality made available from time to time.

Some older pages may use the words "Credits", "token balance", or similar wording. Unless a page says otherwise, those words refer to the same account-linked StudyBase token balance.

2. How tokens can be used

Tokens may be used across supported StudyBase services, including AI tools, processing tasks, enhanced resource access, manual support features, cloud or hosted services, and other premium features.

Not every feature uses tokens, and token-supported features may be added, removed, paused, re-priced, limited, or made free at any time.

3. No fixed cash value

Tokens are not money, currency, cryptocurrency, electronic money, a bank balance, or a cash-equivalent stored value.

Tokens cannot be withdrawn, transferred, sold, exchanged for cash, used outside StudyBase, or treated as having guaranteed future value unless StudyBase explicitly agrees in writing.

Token pack prices and feature costs may change because of AI costs, server costs, third-party service costs, moderation costs, manual workload, demand, or operational requirements.

4. Product codes and top-ups

Tokens may be added by admin-issued product code, manual account adjustment, support-assisted top-up, promotional grant, or another method StudyBase chooses to support. Product codes are usually one-time 8 digit codes.

A code may be refused, cancelled, blocked, or reversed if it is invalid, already used, expired, incorrectly issued, obtained without permission, connected with suspected fraud or abuse, or affected by a technical or administrative error.

5. Failed, cancelled, or disputed requests

If a token-supported request fails because of a technical issue on StudyBase's side, tokens may be refunded or re-credited at StudyBase's discretion. Refunds are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.

Tokens may not be refunded where:

  • the user submits incorrect, incomplete, unclear, or misleading information;
  • the user cancels after processing has started;
  • a third-party service blocks access, changes rules, or becomes unavailable;
  • the request has already started, been partly processed, or been completed;
  • StudyBase reasonably suspects misuse, automation abuse, fraud, chargeback risk, or exploitation.

6. Expiry, inactivity, and availability

Tokens may expire after a period of inactivity or after a stated expiry date if StudyBase introduces expiry rules. Promotional, trial, bonus, gifted, refunded, or manually granted tokens may have different expiry or removal rules from paid tokens.

Buying, receiving, or holding tokens does not guarantee that every token-supported StudyBase feature will always be available.

7. Misuse

Tokens must not be used to abuse, overload, exploit, bypass, attack, reverse engineer, scrape, or interfere with StudyBase or any connected service.

  • Do not duplicate, guess, sell, share, steal, or automate product codes.
  • Do not manipulate balances, requests, device codes, token costs, or redemption records.
  • Do not use chargebacks, false reports, or disputes to obtain free tokens.
  • Do not use tokens for harassment, abuse, unauthorised access, or unlawful requests.

StudyBase may suspend accounts, cancel requests, remove tokens, block product codes, restrict devices, or refuse support where misuse is suspected.

8. Records, younger users, and changes

StudyBase may keep records of token balances, redemptions, product-code status, manual adjustments, support requests, request costs, fraud checks, and account identifiers needed to operate the token system.

If you are under 18, you should only purchase, request, redeem, or use tokens with permission from a parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult where required.

StudyBase may update this Token Policy at any time. Continued purchase, redemption, or use of tokens after updates means you accept the updated policy.

9. Governing law

This Token Policy, and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or connected with it, are intended to be governed by the laws of England and Wales. Subject to mandatory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, the courts of England and Wales shall have jurisdiction.

Nothing in this policy removes statutory consumer, payment, data protection, or other mandatory rights that apply under UK law.
