Overview

1. What StudyBase Credits are

StudyBase Credits are a digital account balance used to access selected StudyBase tools, services, requests, resource features, premium features, or other paid functionality made available from time to time.

The StudyBase interface may use the words "Credits", "tokens", "token balance", or similar wording. Unless a page says otherwise, those words refer to the same account-linked digital balance.

Credits are linked to the StudyBase account email that receives or redeems them. Credits are not linked to a particular device code, and ordinary device-session deletion or weekly device cleanup should not by itself delete the account credit balance.

2. How Credits can be used

Credits can be used across supported StudyBase services, including but not limited to AI tools, humanising tools, homework support tools, cloud gaming, enhanced resource access, processing tasks, manual support features, and other premium features.

Not every feature will use Credits, and not every Credit-supported feature will be available at all times. StudyBase may decide which features require Credits, which features are free, and which features are unavailable.

Before using a paid feature, StudyBase may show an estimated Credit cost. By continuing with that feature or request, you agree that the stated or calculated amount may be deducted from your balance.

3. No fixed cash value

Credits are not money, currency, cryptocurrency, electronic money, a bank balance, a stored-value payment account, or a cash-equivalent balance. They are a limited digital permission to use eligible StudyBase services while those services are available.

Credits cannot be withdrawn, transferred, sold to another user, exchanged for cash, converted back into money, used outside StudyBase, or treated as having a guaranteed future value unless StudyBase explicitly agrees in writing.

Any price shown for a Credit pack only refers to the purchase price or top-up price at the time that pack is offered. It does not guarantee that Credits will always have the same future value, purchasing power, usage rate, or service availability.

4. Prices and usage rates may change

The value, price, usage rate, and spending rules for StudyBase Credits may change at any time. This includes changes to:

  • the number of Credits required for each service or request type;
  • the price of Credit packs or manual top-ups;
  • minimum charges, rounding rules, or per-request fees;
  • bonus Credits, promotional Credits, trial Credits, or pack sizes;
  • service-specific pricing rules, limits, discounts, or availability;
  • the real-world cost comparison between Credits and money.

StudyBase may update Credit pricing or usage rates without prior notice, especially where AI costs, server costs, third-party service costs, moderation costs, manual workload, availability, demand, abuse prevention, or operational requirements change.

5. Service costs and estimates

Different services may cost different amounts of Credits. A service may be priced per AI message, word count, task, file, image, completed request, predicted time, minute, processing step, manual review, or any other pricing method StudyBase reasonably applies.

Some services may have a minimum Credit charge. Some requests may have a variable charge depending on length, complexity, required processing, service availability, third-party restrictions, or the time required to complete the request.

Any estimate shown before a request is an estimate only unless the page clearly says that it is a fixed final price. Final pricing may be adjusted where a task is longer, more complex, unclear, unavailable, manually reviewed, or more expensive to process than expected.

6. Manual and time-based services

Some services may be priced using predicted completion time, estimated task length, queue pressure, manual review, or a direct quote from StudyBase. For example, a homework support or completion-style service may be priced using the predicted completion time shown by the platform or separately estimated by StudyBase.

StudyBase may refuse, cancel, pause, manually quote, or re-price any request where the automatic price is not suitable, the request is unclear, the request is not possible, the service is unavailable, the requested work appears to break rules, or the request would take more time or cost than expected.

7. Product codes and top-ups

Credits may be added by an admin-issued product code, manual account adjustment, support-assisted top-up, promotional grant, or any other method StudyBase chooses to support. Product codes are usually one-time codes and may add a fixed number of Credits to the account that redeems them.

A code may be refused, cancelled, blocked, or reversed if it is invalid, already used, expired, incorrectly issued, obtained without permission, connected with suspected fraud or abuse, or affected by a technical or administrative error.

Users are responsible for entering the correct account email, sending the correct details to StudyBase when requesting a top-up, and keeping product codes private until redeemed.

8. Failed, cancelled, or disputed requests

If a request fails because of a technical issue on StudyBase's side, Credits may be refunded or re-credited at StudyBase's discretion. Refunds are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.

Credits may not be refunded where:

  • the user submits incorrect, incomplete, unclear, or misleading information;
  • the user cancels after processing has started;
  • the user loses connection, closes the page, or cannot receive the result;
  • the user provides invalid login details or invalid third-party information;
  • a third-party website blocks access, changes its rules, or becomes unavailable;
  • the user breaks StudyBase rules, acceptable-use rules, or service-specific instructions;
  • the request has already started, been partly processed, or been completed;
  • StudyBase reasonably suspects misuse, automation abuse, fraud, chargeback risk, or exploitation.

Where real-money payment has been made for Credits, any statutory consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded still apply. Nothing in these rules is intended to remove mandatory rights that apply by law.

9. Expiry, inactivity, and account balance

Credits may expire after a period of inactivity or after a stated expiry date if StudyBase introduces expiry rules. Promotional, trial, bonus, gifted, refunded, or manually granted Credits may have different expiry or removal rules from paid Credits.

StudyBase may remove, freeze, reverse, or adjust Credits from accounts involved in misuse, abuse, fraud, chargebacks, unauthorised access, product-code sharing, attempts to exploit the platform, or administrative mistakes.

If an account is suspended, blocked, deleted, inaccessible, or no longer supported, the associated Credit balance may become unusable unless StudyBase decides otherwise or the law requires a different result.

10. Availability of Credit services

Buying, receiving, or holding Credits does not guarantee that every StudyBase service will always be available. Services may be paused, changed, rate-limited, restricted, removed, or made unavailable due to maintenance, cost changes, abuse prevention, third-party restrictions, legal concerns, moderation, staff availability, or technical issues.

StudyBase may also limit the maximum number of Credits that can be used in a period, require manual approval before a high-cost request, decline a request, or temporarily prevent new Credit spending where needed to protect the service.

11. Misuse and prohibited conduct

Credits must not be used to abuse, overload, exploit, bypass, attack, reverse engineer, scrape, interfere with, or test unauthorised access against StudyBase or any connected service.

Users must not:

  • attempt to duplicate, guess, sell, share, steal, or automate product codes;
  • attempt to manipulate balances, requests, device codes, or Credit costs;
  • use chargebacks, payment disputes, or false reports to obtain free Credits;
  • submit requests intended to break laws, school rules, platform rules, or third-party terms;
  • use Credits for harassment, abuse, harmful content, or unauthorised access.

StudyBase may suspend accounts, cancel requests, remove Credits, block product codes, restrict devices, or refuse support where misuse is suspected.

12. Younger users and permission

StudyBase is commonly used by students. If you are under 18, you should only purchase, request, redeem, or use Credits with permission from a parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult where required.

StudyBase may refuse or reverse Credit activity where it reasonably believes a payment, top-up, request, or account action was not authorised by the account holder or a responsible adult.

13. Records, support, and account data

StudyBase may keep records of Credit balances, redemptions, product-code status, manual adjustments, support requests, request costs, fraud checks, and account identifiers needed to operate the Credit system. This is separate from local browser history, which may stay on the user's device unless a feature explicitly syncs it.

Credit records may be used to investigate errors, support requests, abuse, disputes, chargebacks, account deletion requests, or legal compliance. More information about data handling is available in the main Privacy Policy.

14. Liability and service risk

To the maximum extent permitted by law, StudyBase is not responsible for indirect, consequential, educational, reputational, network, disciplinary, third-party, or opportunity losses connected with Credits or Credit-supported services.

StudyBase does not guarantee that Credits will provide any particular educational outcome, grade improvement, account result, service result, or availability of a third-party destination.

15. Updates to these rules

StudyBase may update these Credit rules at any time. Updated rules may be published on this page, linked from account pages, shown in a notice, or incorporated into the main StudyBase terms.

Continued use, redemption, purchase, or spending of StudyBase Credits after changes are made means you accept the updated rules. If you do not accept the updated rules, you should stop using Credit-supported features and contact StudyBase support where needed.
