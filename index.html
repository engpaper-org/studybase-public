The Revision Cycle

A practical, closed-loop routine.

Don't just practice past papers blindly. Discover weak spots under timed pressure, mark strictly, log topic error codes, and complete target repairs.

01 / Discover

Find the paper

Browse structured exam boards and official subject databases to select papers and official mark schemes.
02 / Execute

Work under pressure

Simulate strict exam conditions with the Command Centre timer to highlight real time management errors.
03 / Log mistakes

Record mistake codes

Mark papers strictly using the examiner criteria. Log the mistake reasons and topic codes inside your mistake inventory.
04 / Repair

Improve weak topics

Use targeted subject tools and simulators to resolve topic conceptual gaps, then re-attempt targeted sets.
