Physics Paper 3

Paper 3 tests practical skills and experimental thinking. Focus on uncertainty, evaluation, and clear method writing.

Focus

Required practicals and data analysis.

Question style

Method design, evaluation, and data processing.

Top mark signal

Quantitative evaluation and precise units.

Checklist

Paper 3 topic checklist

  • Required practical methods and apparatus
  • Variables, controls, and risk assessment
  • Data processing and graph skills
  • Uncertainty, accuracy, and precision
  • Evaluation and improvements
  • Linking results to theory

Exam technique

  • State variables and controls explicitly
  • Use data to justify evaluation points
  • Comment on uncertainty with values
  • Describe improvements that change the method
  • Link results back to equations

Common errors

  • Evaluation without using data
  • Confusing accuracy and precision
  • Missing units on graphs
  • Unclear method steps
  • Ignoring systematic errors

Practice set

  • Design a method to measure the acceleration of a trolley.
  • Interpret a graph and comment on uncertainty.
  • Suggest two improvements to increase reliability.
  • Calculate the gradient and explain its meaning.
