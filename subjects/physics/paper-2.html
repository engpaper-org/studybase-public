Physics Paper 2

Paper 2 moves into fields, particles, and nuclear physics. Expect synoptic questions that pull multiple topics together.

Focus

Fields, electromagnetism, particles, nuclear.

Question style

Multi-topic application and extended calculations.

Top mark signal

Clear assumptions and correct vector directions.

Checklist

Paper 2 topic checklist

  • Gravitational, electric, and magnetic fields
  • Capacitance and electric potential
  • Electromagnetism and motors
  • Particle physics and fundamental forces
  • Nuclear decay and energy
  • Optional topic content (astro, medical, engineering)

Exam technique

  • Sketch field lines with correct direction
  • Use consistent sign conventions
  • Define variables before substituting values
  • Explain assumptions in modelling questions
  • Link particle concepts to evidence

Common errors

  • Mixing field strength with potential
  • Missing negative signs in charge calculations
  • Forgetting energy conservation steps
  • Not stating assumptions about uniform fields
  • Ignoring the optional topic content

Practice set

  • Calculate field strength at a distance from a charge.
  • Find energy stored in a capacitor and interpret.
  • Analyse a particle decay equation.
  • Explain the physics behind an optional topic scenario.
