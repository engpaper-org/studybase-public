Physics Paper 1

Paper 1 tests mechanics, materials, waves, and electricity. Methodical equation use and clear diagrams will secure the marks.

Focus

Motion, forces, waves, circuits, and materials.

Question style

Calculation heavy with short explanations.

Top mark signal

Correct units and fully labeled diagrams.

Checklist

Paper 1 topic checklist

  • Kinematics, forces, momentum, and energy
  • Materials, stress, strain, and elastic behaviour
  • Waves, superposition, diffraction, and optics
  • Electrical circuits and power
  • Wave-particle duality basics
  • Motion graphs and modelling assumptions

Exam technique

  • Sketch force diagrams before calculating
  • Write equations with variable definitions
  • Convert units before substitution
  • Link every step to the physical situation
  • Label wave diagrams with wavelengths and phases

Common errors

  • Ignoring direction in vector quantities
  • Mixing up stress and strain units
  • Forgetting to include power loss or efficiency
  • Using wrong wave equation for the context
  • Not explaining assumptions in modelling questions

Practice set

  • Calculate acceleration from a velocity-time graph.
  • Find Young modulus from data and interpret.
  • Explain diffraction through a narrow slit.
  • Calculate current and power in a mixed circuit.
