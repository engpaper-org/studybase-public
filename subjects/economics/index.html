Economics topic clusters

Group topics into clear micro and macro blocks.

Microeconomics

  • Demand, supply, and elasticity
  • Market structures and competition
  • Market failure and intervention

Macroeconomics

  • Economic growth, inflation, unemployment
  • AD and AS analysis
  • Fiscal, monetary, supply-side policy

Global economics

  • International trade and protectionism
  • Exchange rates and globalisation
  • Development and inequality

  • Data interpretation and context

Exam skills for top marks

  • Draw and label diagrams accurately
  • Explain chains of reasoning clearly
  • Use data to support evaluation
  • Compare policy options with trade-offs
  • Finish essays with a clear judgement

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Unlabelled diagrams
  • Evaluation without evidence
  • Ignoring short and long term effects
  • Repeating the question without analysis
  • Forgetting to define key terms

Quick practice

10 minute drills

Short tasks to sharpen diagrams and evaluation.

Track papers
  • Draw and label a supply and demand shift diagram.
  • Explain the difference between short and long run effects.
  • Analyse a data set and write a conclusion.
  • Evaluate a government policy in two points.
  • Explain one cause of market failure with a diagram.
  • Summarise the macroeconomic objectives in one paragraph.
