Economics Paper 2

Paper 2 focuses on macroeconomics. Show how policies affect the economy in both the short and long run.

Focus

AD/AS, policy, growth, inflation, unemployment.

Question style

Macro data response and evaluation essays.

Top mark signal

Clear diagrams with short and long run analysis.

Checklist

Paper 2 topic checklist

  • Macroeconomic objectives and indicators
  • Aggregate demand and aggregate supply
  • Fiscal, monetary, and supply-side policy
  • International trade and exchange rates
  • Economic growth and productivity
  • Inflation and unemployment causes

Exam technique

  • Label AD and AS diagrams clearly
  • Explain short run vs long run effects
  • Use data to back up evaluation
  • Discuss trade-offs between objectives
  • Finish with a judgement

Common errors

  • Forgetting to include evaluation
  • Unlabelled diagrams
  • Ignoring time period differences
  • Confusing inflation with price levels
  • Not defining key terms

Practice set

  • Explain the impact of a rise in interest rates.
  • Use an AD/AS diagram to show inflation.
  • Evaluate a fiscal policy in two points.
  • Analyse an exchange rate change.

  4. Day 4: Monetary policy
  5. Day 5: Supply-side policy
  6. Day 6: Trade and exchange rates
  7. Day 7: Timed essay practice
