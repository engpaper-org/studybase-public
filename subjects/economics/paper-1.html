Economics Paper 1

Paper 1 covers microeconomics. Master diagrams and evaluation to show how markets respond to change.

Focus

Demand, supply, elasticity, and market failure.

Question style

Data response and diagram-led essays.

Top mark signal

Clear diagrams and balanced evaluation.

Checklist

Paper 1 topic checklist

  • Demand, supply, and market equilibrium
  • Price, income, and cross elasticity
  • Consumer and producer surplus
  • Market structures and competition
  • Market failure and externalities
  • Government intervention and evaluation

Exam technique

  • Draw diagrams with labels and shifts
  • Explain the direction of change clearly
  • Evaluate policies with pros and cons
  • Use data in your evaluation
  • Finish with a judgement

Common errors

  • Unlabelled diagrams
  • Explaining without evaluation
  • Confusing types of elasticity
  • Ignoring time scales
  • No conclusion or judgement

Practice set

  • Draw an elasticity diagram and explain the change.
  • Evaluate a tax policy with two pros and cons.
  • Analyse a subsidy using a diagram.
  • Explain how market power affects efficiency.
