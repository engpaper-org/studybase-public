English Literature Paper 1

Paper 1 typically focuses on drama or Shakespeare plus poetry. Prioritise close analysis and strong quotation use.

Focus

Drama or Shakespeare, plus poetry analysis.

Question style

Close reading and thematic essays.

Top mark signal

Precise quotations and analysis.

Checklist

Paper 1 checklist

  • Key scenes and turning points
  • Character development and relationships
  • Core themes and motifs
  • Language, structure, and form analysis
  • Context and critical viewpoints
  • Quotation bank for key themes

Exam technique

  • Embed short quotations and analyse them
  • Use topic sentences that link to the question
  • Compare interpretations if required
  • Balance language and context in each paragraph
  • Finish with a strong judgement

Common errors

  • Plot summary without analysis
  • Long quotes without explanation
  • Weak introduction with no argument
  • Ignoring the question wording
  • Rushed conclusion

Practice set

  • Analyse a short extract for language and tone.
  • Plan an essay on a central theme.
  • Compare two characters in one paragraph.
  • Write a conclusion with judgement.

7 day sprint plan

  1. Day 1: Key scenes and summaries
  2. Day 2: Character profiles and quotes
  3. Day 3: Themes and motifs
  4. Day 4: Language and structure analysis
  5. Day 5: Context and critics
  6. Day 6: Essay planning drills
  7. Day 7: Timed essay practice
