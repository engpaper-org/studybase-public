English Literature Paper 2

Paper 2 often includes prose, comparative writing, and unseen analysis. Focus on structure, theme links, and argument flow.

Focus

Prose, comparison, and unseen texts.

Question style

Comparative essays and unseen analysis.

Top mark signal

Clear comparisons and critical debate.

Checklist

Paper 2 checklist

  • Comparative themes across texts
  • Key quotations and passages
  • Unseen poetry analysis practice
  • Critical perspectives and debate
  • Contextual links for each text
  • Essay structure for comparisons

Exam technique

  • Compare texts in every paragraph
  • Use short quotations from both texts
  • Discuss how form and structure shape meaning
  • Link context to interpretation
  • End with a clear judgement

Common errors

  • Writing about one text only
  • Long quotations without analysis
  • Weak links between themes
  • Ignoring the question wording
  • No conclusion or judgement

Practice set

  • Write a comparison paragraph on a shared theme.
  • Analyse an unseen poem for language and structure.
  • Plan an essay with a clear thesis statement.
  • Summarise a critical viewpoint in two sentences.

7 day sprint plan

  1. Day 1: Prose text summary and quotes
  2. Day 2: Comparative themes practice
  3. Day 3: Unseen analysis drills
  4. Day 4: Context and critical debate
  5. Day 5: Essay planning practice
  6. Day 6: Timed comparative essay
  7. Day 7: Review feedback and refine
