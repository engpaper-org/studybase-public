Core topics

English Literature skill clusters

Plan revision around both text knowledge and essay skills.

Close reading

  • Language, structure, and form analysis
  • Quotation selection and embedding
  • Multiple interpretations

Context and critics

  • Historical and social context
  • Critical viewpoints and debate
  • Authorial intention discussion

Comparative writing

  • Comparing themes and methods
  • Linking texts with coherent transitions
  • Balanced evaluation

Essay structure

  • Clear thesis statements
  • Paragraph structure and argument flow
  • Judgement and conclusion

Exam skills for top marks

  • Embed short quotations and analyse them
  • Use topic sentences to guide the argument
  • Compare texts directly in each paragraph
  • Write with a clear critical voice
  • Finish with a concise judgement

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Retelling the plot
  • Long quotations with no analysis
  • One-sided arguments without debate
  • Ignoring context where required
  • Weak conclusion
Revision flow

A simple 3 stage plan

Balance text knowledge, analysis, and essay practice.

Build a timetable

3. Timed essays

Write full essays with a clear line of argument.

Quick practice

10 minute drills

Short tasks to sharpen analysis and essay structure.

Track papers
  • Analyse a short quotation for language and effect.
  • Write a thesis statement for a given question.
  • Compare a theme across two texts in four sentences.
  • Summarise a critical viewpoint in your own words.
  • Create a paragraph plan using point, evidence, analysis.
  • Write a conclusion with judgement.
