Exam skills for top marks

  • Show working and units for every calculation
  • Use curly arrows for mechanisms
  • State reagents, conditions, and observations
  • Balance equations and check oxidation states
  • Quote data points in evaluation questions

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Mixing up rate and equilibrium concepts
  • Forgetting state symbols or charges
  • Missing a step in multi-stage synthesis
  • Incorrect significant figures
  • Ignoring conditions like temperature or catalyst

Quick practice

10 minute drills

Short sessions that sharpen calculations and reaction memory.

Track papers
  • Balance two redox equations and identify oxidising agents.
  • Complete a titration calculation with correct units.
  • Sketch a reaction profile and label activation energy.
  • Identify functional groups from IR and NMR clues.
  • Write a mechanism with curly arrows and conditions.
  • Summarise the trends across a periodic group.
