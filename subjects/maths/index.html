Exam skills for top marks

  • Show full method lines even if you can do it mentally
  • Sketch graphs with key points and intercepts labelled
  • Check domains, ranges, and restrictions before solving
  • Use exact values unless told to round
  • Write clear assumptions for modelling questions
  • Estimate to sanity check every answer

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Sign errors when rearranging or differentiating
  • Mixing up degrees and radians
  • Forgetting constants of integration
  • Using the wrong tail in hypothesis tests
  • Ignoring units or vector direction
  • Stopping after a numeric answer without context

Track papers
  • Solve a proof question using algebraic manipulation and clear structure.
  • Complete a 5 mark calculus application and verify with units.
  • Interpret a normal distribution question and write a conclusion in context.
  • Model a particle on a slope with a clear force diagram.
  • Sketch a function and annotate roots, asymptotes, and turning points.
  • Write a short error log from your last timed paper.
