Pure Mathematics

Pure Maths is where method marks are won. Focus on clean algebra, exact values, and clear reasoning in every step.

Focus

Algebra, functions, trig, sequences, and calculus.

Question style

Multi-step proofs, modelling, and graph interpretation.

Top mark signal

Exact answers, clear structure, and annotated diagrams.

Checklist

Pure Maths topic checklist

  • Algebraic manipulation, factorising, partial fractions
  • Functions, inverses, composite functions, transformations
  • Sequences and series, proof by induction
  • Trigonometric identities, equations, and graphs
  • Differentiation and application to rates and optimisation
  • Integration techniques and area under curves
  • Vectors in 2D and 3D, line and plane geometry
  • Numerical methods and iterative formulas

Exam technique

  • Write every algebra step, even for easy rearrangements
  • Use exact trig values before switching to decimals
  • Label graphs with intercepts and key turning points
  • State all assumptions in modelling questions
  • Check domains and ranges before final answers

Common errors

  • Dropping negative signs during differentiation
  • Mixing radians and degrees in trig questions
  • Forgetting constants of integration
  • Using invalid algebraic cancellation
  • Stopping without stating the final conclusion

Practice set

  • Prove a trig identity by transforming one side only.
  • Differentiate a composite function and find stationary points.
  • Model a real situation with a differential equation and solve it.
  • Use vectors to find the angle between two lines.
