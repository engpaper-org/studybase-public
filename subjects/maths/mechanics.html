Mechanics

Mechanics rewards clear diagrams, careful sign conventions, and well-structured calculations. Treat every question like a physics story with a clean model.

Focus

Kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, and moments.

Question style

Particles, projectiles, and modelling with equations.

Top mark signal

Correct diagrams, units, and defined directions.

Checklist

Mechanics topic checklist

  • Constant acceleration and SUVAT equations
  • Forces, free body diagrams, and Newton laws
  • Projectiles and motion in two dimensions
  • Moments, equilibrium, and centers of mass
  • Work, energy, and power relationships
  • Impulse and conservation of momentum
  • Friction, connected particles, and pulleys

Exam technique

  • Always draw and label a free body diagram
  • Define a positive direction before solving
  • Check units at every step of a calculation
  • State assumptions such as smooth surfaces
  • Link equations to the story of the problem

Common errors

  • Mixing up speed and velocity
  • Incorrect force direction or sign
  • Forgetting components on a slope
  • Using the wrong SUVAT equation
  • Missing units in the final answer

Practice set

  • Solve a particle on an inclined plane with friction.
  • Model a projectile and calculate range and time.
  • Use conservation of momentum in a collision.
  • Apply work and energy to a connected particles question.
