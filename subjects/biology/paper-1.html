Biology Paper 1

Paper 1 is the foundation of the course. Build strong recall of key processes, then link structure to function in every response.

Focus

Biological molecules, cells, genetics, and transport.

Question style

Process explanations, diagrams, and data interpretation.

Top mark signal

Precise terminology and step-by-step reasoning.

Checklist

Paper 1 topic checklist

  • Water, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and enzymes
  • Cell structure, microscopy, and cell membranes
  • Transport across membranes and exchange surfaces
  • DNA structure, replication, and protein synthesis
  • Cell cycle, mitosis, and meiosis
  • Genetics, variation, and inheritance patterns
  • Immune responses and pathogens

Exam technique

  • Describe processes in the correct sequence
  • Link structure to function with clear evidence
  • Use key terms such as hydrolysis or diffusion
  • Quote data points when interpreting graphs
  • Label diagrams neatly and accurately

Common errors

  • Confusing transcription with translation
  • Mixing active transport with diffusion
  • Using vague terms like bigger or smaller
  • Not linking explanation back to the question
  • Omitting units in calculations

Practice set

  • Explain the effect of temperature on enzyme activity.
  • Describe the steps of DNA replication.
  • Interpret a graph of membrane transport rate.
  • Analyse a genetic cross and calculate ratios.
