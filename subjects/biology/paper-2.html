Biology Paper 2

Paper 2 connects big systems with real outcomes. Prioritise clear chains of reasoning and link topics together.

Focus

Respiration, photosynthesis, homeostasis, ecology.

Question style

Long explanations, data response, and evaluation.

Top mark signal

Accurate sequences and key terminology in context.

Checklist

Paper 2 topic checklist

  • Photosynthesis and respiration pathways
  • Energy transfer and ATP
  • Nervous and hormonal coordination
  • Homeostasis and feedback loops
  • Populations, ecosystems, and succession
  • Inheritance of populations and evolution
  • Responses to environmental change

Exam technique

  • Build chain explanations with clear steps
  • Use correct terms like increase, inhibit, regulate
  • Describe feedback using stimulus and response
  • Link data trends to biological reasoning
  • Use comparative language when asked to evaluate

Common errors

  • Confusing negative and positive feedback
  • Missing steps in respiration or photosynthesis
  • Ignoring the ecological context in questions
  • Writing vague conclusions without data
  • Forgetting to state the direction of change

Practice set

  • Explain how light intensity affects photosynthesis rate.
  • Describe the roles of insulin and glucagon.
  • Interpret an energy pyramid and explain losses.
  • Evaluate data from a population study.
