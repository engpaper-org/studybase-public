Back to Psychology

Psychology Paper 1

Paper 1 is all about classic topics and core studies. Prepare clear AO1 notes and balanced evaluation points.

Focus

Social influence, memory, attachment, psychopathology.

Question style

Short answers and extended essays.

Top mark signal

Named studies and clear evaluation.

Checklist

Paper 1 topic checklist

  • Social influence, obedience, and conformity
  • Memory models and forgetting
  • Attachment theories and studies
  • Psychopathology definitions and treatments
  • Key studies, aims, procedures, findings
  • Evaluation points for every topic

Exam technique

  • Use AO1 then AO3 structure in essays
  • Quote named studies with key findings
  • Balance strengths and limitations
  • Use comparative language where needed
  • Define key terms before applying them

Common errors

  • Describing without evaluating
  • Mixing up theories or studies
  • Failing to link to the question
  • Writing a conclusion without judgement
  • Missing key terms in definitions

Practice set

  • Outline a study on obedience and evaluate it.
  • Compare two explanations of forgetting.
  • Explain one attachment theory using evidence.
  • Evaluate a treatment for depression.
