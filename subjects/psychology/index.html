Core topics

Psychology topic clusters

Group topics to support essay planning.

Core topics

  • Social influence, memory, attachment
  • Psychopathology and approaches
  • Key studies and evaluation

Research methods

  • Experiments, observations, and questionnaires
  • Sampling, ethics, validity, reliability
  • Descriptive and inferential statistics

Biopsychology

  • Neurons, synapses, and brain areas
  • Endocrine system and stress response
  • Biological rhythms and sleep

Options and debates

  • Relationships, cognition, or schizophrenia
  • Issues and debates in psychology
  • Comparing theories and treatments

Exam skills for top marks

  • Use AO1, AO2, AO3 structure in essays
  • Quote named studies with key findings
  • Balance strengths and limitations in evaluation
  • Define key terms clearly before applying
  • Use research methods to support arguments

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Missing the evaluation part of the essay
  • Describing a study without linking to the question
  • Forgetting research method terminology
  • Not comparing theories in depth
  • Writing conclusions without judgement

Quick practice

10 minute drills

Quick tasks to sharpen AO1 and AO3 skills.

Track papers
  • Write a 60 second summary of a key study.
  • List two strengths and two limitations of an experiment.
  • Apply a theory to a real-life scenario in four sentences.
  • Explain a research method and when it is best used.
  • Compare two theories using a short paragraph.
  • Define a command word and give an example answer.
