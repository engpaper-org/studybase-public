Back to Psychology

Psychology Paper 3

Paper 3 covers option topics and synoptic evaluation. Blend AO1 knowledge with strong AO3 analysis.

Focus

Option topics and issues and debates.

Question style

Extended essays and evaluation-heavy questions.

Top mark signal

Balanced evaluation and clear judgement.

Checklist

Paper 3 topic checklist

  • Option topic theories and studies
  • Applications and treatments
  • Issues and debates across psychology
  • Research methods applied to options
  • Comparisons of explanations
  • Evidence-based evaluation

Exam technique

  • Plan essays with AO1, AO2, AO3 balance
  • Use named studies as evidence
  • Compare theories directly in evaluation
  • Include real-world application examples
  • Finish with a judgement or conclusion

Common errors

  • Listing points without evaluation
  • Mixing option topics in one answer
  • Failing to apply research methods
  • Ignoring issues and debates
  • Ending without judgement

Practice set

  • Evaluate one theory from your option topic.
  • Compare two treatments for a disorder.
  • Apply issues and debates to a named study.
  • Plan a 16 mark essay in five bullet points.
