Psychology Paper 2

Paper 2 focuses on research methods, approaches, and biopsychology. Precision with terminology and statistics is essential.

Focus

Research methods, approaches, biopsychology.

Question style

Short answer, methods, and data analysis.

Top mark signal

Correct terminology and statistical reasoning.

Checklist

Paper 2 topic checklist

  • Research methods and experimental design
  • Variables, sampling, ethics, validity
  • Descriptive and inferential statistics
  • Approaches: cognitive, behavioural, biological
  • Biopsychology and the nervous system
  • Issues and debates in psychology

Exam technique

  • Define key terms before applying them
  • Use correct statistical language
  • Justify choice of research method
  • Use evidence to support issues and debates
  • Show calculations clearly for data questions

Common errors

  • Confusing validity and reliability
  • Using the wrong statistical test
  • Missing ethical considerations
  • Not linking issues to the question
  • Incorrect interpretation of data

Practice set

  • Design an experiment and identify controls.
  • Select a statistical test and justify it.
  • Explain the role of the synapse in transmission.
  • Evaluate a research method with two strengths.
