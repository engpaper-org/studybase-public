History Paper 2

Paper 2 is your depth study. Focus on detailed knowledge and clear explanations of causation and consequence.

Focus

Depth study and thematic evaluation.

Question style

Extended essays and thematic analysis.

Top mark signal

Detailed evidence and clear judgement.

Checklist

Paper 2 topic checklist

  • Key events and turning points
  • Causation, consequence, and significance
  • Short and long-term change
  • Key individuals and leadership decisions
  • Debates and interpretations
  • Essay structure and judgement

Exam technique

  • Use point, evidence, analysis in every paragraph
  • Explain why factors are more or less significant
  • Show links across time periods
  • Keep the essay question in every paragraph
  • Finish with a clear judgement

Common errors

  • Listing events without explanation
  • Ignoring the time period boundaries
  • Unbalanced argument with no judgement
  • Overly narrative paragraphs
  • Missing links between factors

Practice set

  • Plan an essay on causes with three factors.
  • Write a paragraph on significance using evidence.
  • Compare two interpretations in four sentences.
  • Draft a conclusion with judgement.

7 day sprint plan

  1. Day 1: Key events and timeline
  2. Day 2: Causes and consequences
  3. Day 3: Key individuals and roles
  4. Day 4: Interpretation debates
  5. Day 5: Essay planning and structure
  6. Day 6: Timed essay practice
  7. Day 7: Review feedback and refine
