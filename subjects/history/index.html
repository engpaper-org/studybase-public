Core topics

History skill clusters

Plan revision around skills as well as content.

Knowledge building

  • Chronologies and key turning points
  • Key individuals and policies
  • Context and thematic change

Source skills

  • Provenance and reliability
  • Interpretation and bias
  • Comparing sources

Essay construction

  • Clear line of argument
  • Balanced evaluation
  • Judgement in every paragraph

Coursework mastery

  • Research question framing
  • Using evidence effectively
  • Academic writing and referencing

Exam skills for top marks

  • Start essays with a clear argument
  • Use evidence to support every paragraph
  • Weigh up interpretations, not just describe
  • Make judgement explicit in the conclusion
  • Use provenance to evaluate sources

Common pitfalls to avoid

  • Narrative writing without analysis
  • Ignoring the question focus
  • Weak links between evidence and judgement
  • Unbalanced evaluation of interpretations
  • Missing context in source analysis

Quick practice

10 minute drills

Short tasks to sharpen evidence and judgement.

Track papers
  • Write a 3 sentence judgement on a key debate.
  • Summarise a source and assess its reliability.
  • Create a timeline of five turning points.
  • Draft an essay intro with a clear argument.
  • List two consequences of a key event.
  • Plan a paragraph using point, evidence, analysis.
