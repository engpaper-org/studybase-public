History Paper 1

Paper 1 blends breadth knowledge with source evaluation. Balance narrative detail with judgement.

Focus

Breadth study, interpretations, and sources.

Question style

Essays and source analysis.

Top mark signal

Provenance and balanced judgement.

Checklist

Paper 1 topic checklist

  • Chronology and key turning points
  • Long term change and continuity
  • Interpretations and debates
  • Source provenance and utility
  • Essay structure with judgement
  • Key individuals and policies

Exam technique

  • Start with a clear line of argument
  • Use evidence to support every claim
  • Evaluate source reliability using provenance
  • Compare interpretations directly
  • Finish with a clear judgement

Common errors

  • Describing without analysis
  • Ignoring the question focus
  • Using evidence without evaluation
  • Not linking sources to context
  • Weak conclusions

Practice set

  • Plan a judgement-led essay in five bullet points.
  • Evaluate the reliability of a source in context.
  • Compare two interpretations on a key debate.
  • Write an intro paragraph with an argument.

  1. Day 1: Timeline and key events

  3. Day 3: Interpretations and debates
  4. Day 4: Source analysis practice
  5. Day 5: Essay planning and structure
  6. Day 6: Timed essay practice
  7. Day 7: Review feedback and refine
