History Coursework

Coursework is your chance to build a detailed argument with real sources. Choose a focused question and be consistent with evidence.

Focus

Independent investigation and analysis.

Key skill

Using evidence to build an argument.

Top mark signal

Clear structure and balanced evaluation.

Checklist

Coursework checklist

  • Clear research question with a focused scope
  • Balanced set of primary and secondary sources
  • Organised structure with chapters or themes
  • Evidence used to support every claim
  • Evaluation of interpretations and reliability
  • Consistent referencing and bibliography

Coursework technique

  • Set a clear line of argument from the start
  • Use evidence and provenance to justify claims
  • Balance different interpretations fairly
  • Summarise findings at the end of each section
  • Keep your writing focused on the question

Common errors

  • Topic too broad to manage
  • Heavy narrative without analysis
  • Over-reliance on one source type
  • Weak referencing and missing citations
  • Unclear conclusion without judgement

Practice set

  • Draft a research question in one sentence.
  • Summarise two sources and compare reliability.
  • Write a paragraph with evidence and evaluation.
  • Plan a conclusion with clear judgement.

7 day sprint plan

  1. Day 1: Finalise the research question
  2. Day 2: Collect and summarise sources
  3. Day 3: Build a detailed plan
  4. Day 4: Draft the first section
  5. Day 5: Draft the second section
  6. Day 6: Write conclusion and evaluation
  7. Day 7: Edit, reference, and polish
