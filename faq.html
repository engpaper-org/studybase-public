FAQ

Answers to the questions students actually ask.

Clear information about using the revision workspace, safety systems, and study tools.

Is StudyBase only for A-Level students?

Yes — the platform is built specifically around A-Level paper practice, mark schemes, and focused repair work for Year 12 and Year 13.

Do I need to log in to use anything?

No. Many tools (focus timer, checklists, flashcards, past paper browsing) work completely without an account. Login unlocks the full protected resource vault and personal progress features.

What are the safety features?

Automatic wellbeing breaks, daily healthy-use limits, real-time content safety filters on search/inputs, one-click reporting, and a full set of student support resources.

Where do I find study tools like the timer or planner?

Go to the Study Tools hub. Everything saves locally in your browser and works offline.

How do I report a problem or dead link?

Use the Report page or Contact form. Include the exact page URL and what happened.

Is my data private?

Most study tools (timer, planner, checklists) never leave your device. Account features use approved access checks. Full details are in the Privacy policy.
