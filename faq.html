Platform & Account

Is this site for GCSE or A-Level?

+
Both! We have split the platform into two distinct portals. You can toggle between the GCSE Portal (Year 10-11) and the A-Level Portal (Year 12-13) using the navigation menu or the homepage selection cards.

Do I need to pay for resources?

+
No. All core revision materials—including past papers, mark schemes, and summary notes—are completely free. We believe education should be accessible to everyone.

GCSE Support

Are the papers updated for 2026?

+
Yes. We regularly update our database to align with the latest 2026 specifications for AQA, Edexcel, OCR, and Eduqas. If a specification changes, we flag old resources as "Legacy".

Do you have Foundation Tier content?

+
Absolutely. For Maths and Sciences, you can filter resources by 'Foundation' or 'Higher' tier to ensure you aren't revising irrelevant content.

A-Level & UCAS

Does the site help with Personal Statements?

+
Yes, visit the UCAS Hub. We have a breakdown of how to structure your statement, examples of successful Oxbridge applications, and super-curricular reading lists.

What is the "Focus Room"?

+
The Focus Room is a shared tool for both GCSE and A-Level students. It provides a distraction-free interface with Pomodoro timers, lofi music, and task tracking to help you complete "Deep Work" sessions.
