FAQ

Quick answers for the remastered site.

StudyBase is now focused on A-Level papers, useful tools and simple access checking.

Is StudyBase for A-Level students?

Yes. The remastered public pages are written around A-Level paper practice and revision planning.

Is there a paid balance system?

No. The remastered flow is based on login and approved email access.

How does access work?

Access checks are based on whether your email is already on the approved list from the form.

Where are study tools?

Use Study Tools for planning, timing, tracking and recall routes.

What should I do first?

Choose a subject, attempt a paper, mark it, then record one repair task.

Can I report a dead link?

Yes. Use the report page or contact page and include the page URL.
