# StudyBase Site Suggestions

A living list of feature, content, and improvement ideas for the StudyBase platform.

Last updated: 2026

---

## New Study Tools

- **AI Study Buddy / Question Explainer**  
  Allow students to paste a past paper question or textbook paragraph and get a clear, age-appropriate explanation + follow-up questions.

- **Spaced Repetition Flashcard System**  
  Built-in Anki-style system that tracks performance over time, with subject-specific decks and progress analytics.

- **Personalised Revision Timetable Generator**  
  Takes a student's subjects, exam dates, and current confidence levels to produce a realistic daily/weekly plan.

- **Essay Feedback Tool (Human + AI)**  
  Students upload practice essays (especially humanities) and receive structured feedback based on mark schemes.

- **Live Exam Simulator**  
  Full timed mock exams with automatic marking for objective questions and suggested model answers for written ones.

- **Focus & Pomodoro with Accountability**  
  Enhanced focus timer that can pair with friends or show "study with me" live sessions.

---

## Content & Resources

- **GCSE-First Content Expansion**  
  While A-Level is strong, many users start earlier. Prioritise GCSE past papers, topic guides, and tools.

- **Video Explanation Library**  
  Short, high-quality videos for tricky topics (user-submitted + curated). Could live in the resource database.

- **"Common Mistakes" Database**  
  Crowdsourced or moderator-curated list of frequent errors per topic, with correct methods shown.

- **Specification Tracker**  
  Tool that shows exactly which parts of the spec a student has covered vs still needs work.

- **University & Career Pathway Guides**  
  Subject-specific pages showing what degrees/careers are common for each A-Level combination.

---

## Wellbeing & Support

- **Daily/Weekly Mood & Study Check-in**  
  Simple private check-in that shows trends over time (study hours vs wellbeing) without being intrusive.

- **"Study Buddy" Matching**  
  Opt-in system to be paired with other students studying similar subjects for accountability and support.

- **Burnout Prevention Alerts**  
  Gentle, data-driven nudges when a user has been studying intensely for long periods without breaks.

- **More Safety Banner Variants**  
  Additional rotating support messages (e.g. exam stress, friendship issues, body image, etc.).

---

## Community & Social

- **Public Study Notes Sharing**  
  Students can publish high-quality revision notes (with moderation) and earn contribution points.

- **Subject-Specific Discussion Boards**  
  Moderated forums per subject (with clear rules and quick-exit features).

- **"Revision Challenge" Leaderboards**  
  Opt-in weekly challenges (e.g. "Complete 50 past paper questions this week").

---

## Account & Personalisation

- **Proper Progress Dashboard**  
  Visual overview of resources accessed, tools used, time spent, and weak areas across subjects.

- **Customisable Homepage**  
  Logged-in users can pin their most-used tools and subjects.

- **Export Everything**  
  One-click export of all personal data (notes, timers, history, settings) as a zip.

- **Multiple Device Sync**  
  Better session handling so progress in tools carries across phone, tablet, and laptop.

---

## Technical / Platform

- **Progressive Web App (PWA) Improvements**  
  Better offline support for key tools (flashcards, planners, timers).

- **Keyboard Shortcuts Everywhere**  
  Power users love shortcuts. Add a discoverable shortcut system.

- **Better Search Across the Whole Site**  
  Unified search that surfaces resources, tools, blog posts, and support articles.

- **Accessibility Audit & Improvements**  
  Ensure full WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, especially for support features and tools.

- **Dark Mode Polish**  
  Consistent dark theme across all tools, legal pages, and the resource viewer.

---

## Growth & Outreach

- **Teacher / School Portal**  
  Simple way for teachers to recommend specific resources or track class progress (privacy-first).

- **Ambassador Programme**  
  Students can earn perks for promoting StudyBase responsibly within their schools/sixth forms.

- **"StudyBase for GCSE" Landing Page**  
  Dedicated marketing for the younger audience.

---

## How to Use This List

- Feel free to add new ideas at the bottom with a short description.
- When an idea is implemented, move it to an "Implemented" section or delete it.
- Prioritise based on user feedback, safety considerations, and development effort.
