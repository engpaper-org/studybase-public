Mistake Log

Make every lost mark useful.

A mistake log should be short and specific: paper, question, topic, error type, why it happened and what you will do before the next attempt.

Log format

Paper: Edexcel Maths 2024 P1. Topic: integration. Error: method choice. Fix: redo three mixed integration questions before the next full paper.

Best next action

When the same topic appears twice in your log, stop doing full papers for a moment and repair that topic deliberately.
