Site Update

More resources, a better design, and a stronger A-Level focus

We’ve updated the site to make StudyBase feel cleaner, more focused, and more useful for A-Level students.

This update brings a wider range of resources across key subjects, alongside a refreshed design that feels more polished and easier to use.

We’re also continuing to shape the platform around A-Level revision, with a clearer focus on Years 12 and 13, deeper content, and a more premium overall experience.

Over time, you can expect the library to keep expanding with more materials, better organisation, and a stronger student dashboard experience.
